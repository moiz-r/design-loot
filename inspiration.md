# Inspiration

Cool websites for ideas: layouts, brands, motion, and UI tricks.

| Name | What it is | Why it is cool |
|---|---|---|
| [Rico UI — Brands](https://design.ricoui.com/brands) | Gallery of brands and UI designs | See many brands side by side. Good for visual direction |
| [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/) | 2000+ design styles from big product sites, as DESIGN.md files | Pick a style you like, then hand it to your AI tools |

| [Wafer](https://www.wafer.ai/) | Fast AI inference cloud for open models. The product is not design | Saved for the site itself: a good example of how an AI company presents itself |
| [TypeSafe AI](https://typesafe.ai/) | "System One" models: fast, typed decisions instead of chat text. The product is not design | Saved for the site itself: interesting way to explain a hard product simply |
| [Rho](https://www.rho.co/) | Fintech: business banking and corporate cards for startups | Polished brand site. Good reference for SaaS and fintech pages |
| [Plasma One](https://www.plasma.org/personal) | Stablecoin card and currency account for everyday money | Clean product page. Good reference for fintech and crypto UI |
